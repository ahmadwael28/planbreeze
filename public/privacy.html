Planbreeze

Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Planbreeze is a free web app for drawing floor plans, furnishing them and seeing them in 3D, at ahmadwael28.github.io/planbreeze. Its source code is public on GitHub. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Without an account

You can use Planbreeze without signing in. Your plans, settings, and any photos or drawings you add are saved in your browser's storage on your own device. They are not sent to us.

With an account

If you sign in with Google, GitHub or an email link, so your plans are kept in the cloud and on all your devices:

  • Account details. We receive your email address and, from Google or GitHub, your name, profile picture and an account identifier. We use these only to sign you in and to show which account is signed in.
  • Your plans. We store the plans you save, including any photos and background drawings you add to them, so you can open them on any device. Only your account can read or change them.

Sign-in and storage are provided by Supabase, with the database hosted in the European Union. Your session is kept in your browser's storage so you stay signed in.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, Planbreeze asks only for your basic profile: your name, email address and profile picture. We use it only to sign you in. We do not use it for advertising, sell it, or share it with anyone, and we do not use it to train AI models. Planbreeze's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no selling or renting of your information.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts, and no tracking cookies.

Other services

  • GitHub Pages serves the website. Like any web host, it may log technical details such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google and GitHub handle sign-in when you choose them, under their own privacy policies.
  • Anthropic, only if you use the optional AI import with your own API key: the picture you import is sent from your browser directly to Anthropic to read the plan. Your key stays in your browser.

Keeping and deleting your information

Plans stay in your account until you delete them; deleting a plan removes it from the cloud. Plans saved only in your browser are removed when you clear your browser's site data. To delete your account and everything stored with it, contact us on GitHub. You don't need to post personal details there; we'll reply to arrange it and delete it within 30 days.

Children

Planbreeze is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
